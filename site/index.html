Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag HeyNav to Applications.
  2. Launch it. macOS will block it once — dismiss the warning.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.
Why does macOS block it?

Because HeyNav isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account. macOS says the same thing about every app that isn't — it's not a judgement about the app itself.

HeyNav is free and the source is public, so there's no account behind it. You can read every line, or build it yourself and skip this entirely.

If you'd rather use the terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/HeyNav.app

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no telemetry. HeyNav is sandboxed and asks for calendar access only to read your next event, and network access only to fetch weather from Open-Meteo. Decline either — the rest keeps working.

Updates

HeyNav can't update itself — unsigned apps aren't allowed to. When a new version lands, download it and drag it over the old one. Your settings are kept.
